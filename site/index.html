All FAQs
Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with multiple lenders. Fill out our online credit application and we’ll look for options that fit your situation. Financing is subject to lender approval, and rates and terms depend on the lender and your credit.

Can I apply with bad credit or no credit history?

Yes, you’re welcome to apply. Our lenders work with all types of credit scores and situations, including first-time buyers. Every application is reviewed by the lender, so approval isn’t guaranteed, but applying is how you find out what’s possible.

Can I apply with a co-buyer?

Yes. The credit application has a separate Co-Buyer section, so you and a co-applicant can apply together.

What if I don’t see the car I want?

Use our Car Finder. Tell us the make, model, year, mileage, and price range you want, and we’ll help you look for it through our dealer sources, including auctions.

Do the listed prices include fees?

Our online listings note that dealer fees are not disclosed there, so please contact us to confirm the full out-the-door price before you buy. Taxes, title, and registration are extra.

Where is SA Innovative World located?

We’re at 6829 Lankershim Blvd, Unit 226, North Hollywood, CA 91605, on Lankershim Blvd in the San Fernando Valley. Call (626) 764-5340 before you head over so we can have your car ready.
