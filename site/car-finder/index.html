FAQ

Car Finder questions

What if I don’t see the car I want?

Use our Car Finder. Tell us the make, model, year, mileage, and price range you want, and we’ll help you look for it through our dealer sources, including auctions.

How will you contact me?

We’ll reach out using the email or phone number you provide on the form. Text messages are opt-in only; reply STOP to opt out.

Can I apply for financing while you search?

Yes. Applying early means you’ll know your options when we find a match. Financing is subject to lender approval.
