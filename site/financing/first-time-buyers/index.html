FAQ

First-time buyer questions

Can I finance a car with no credit history?

Yes, first-time buyers are welcome to apply. With no credit history, lenders look more closely at income, time at your job, where you live, and your down payment. Approval isn’t guaranteed.

Do I need a co-signer?

Not always. A co-buyer with established credit can strengthen an application, and our credit application has a co-buyer section if you want to add one.

What documents should I bring?

Bring your driver’s license, proof of income such as recent pay stubs, proof of address, and proof of insurance. Call us and we’ll confirm what your lender needs.

What do I need to fill out the credit application?

Have your contact details, date of birth, Social Security number, driver’s license, current address and housing payment, and employer and monthly income handy. You can also list the vehicle you’re interested in, your down payment, and a trade-in.

Do your cars come with a warranty?

It depends on the vehicle, so ask us about the one you’re interested in. Every used car offered by a dealer has a federal Buyers Guide on the window that says whether it’s sold with a warranty or “as is.”
