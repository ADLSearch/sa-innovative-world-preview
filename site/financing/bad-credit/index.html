FAQ

Bad credit questions

Can I get a car loan with bad credit?

You can apply. Our lenders work with all types of credit scores and situations. Every application is reviewed by the lender, so approval isn’t guaranteed, and rates for lower scores are usually higher.

Will applying hurt my credit?

Lenders who review your application may pull your credit report, which can show up as an inquiry. Ask us if you have questions before you apply.

Does a bigger down payment help?

Often, yes. A larger down payment lowers the amount you borrow, which lenders tend to view favorably and which lowers your monthly payment.

Can I apply with a co-buyer?

Yes. The credit application has a separate Co-Buyer section, so you and a co-applicant can apply together.

Can I trade in my current car?

The credit application lets you add a trade-in vehicle (VIN, year, make, model, and mileage). For questions about your trade, call us at (626) 764-5340.
