Our story

The right fit, at a
fair price.

SA Innovative World is a pre-owned car dealership on Lankershim Blvd in North Hollywood. We’re dedicated to offering quality pre-owned vehicles, and from the moment you walk through our door, we’re committed to giving you a great car-buying experience.

We work with sources across the dealer community to find a wide variety of vehicles, and we offer financing through multiple lenders. Whether you have good credit, bad credit, or you’re buying your first car, we’ll help you find the vehicle that’s the right fit.

Browse our inventory online. If you see a vehicle you like, request a quote or contact us to schedule a test drive. To learn more about how we can help with your next car, call or stop by in person. We look forward to meeting you.
