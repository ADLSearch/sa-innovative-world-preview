About us

The right fit, at a fair price

SA Innovative World is a pre-owned car dealership at 6829 Lankershim Blvd, Unit 226, North Hollywood, CA 91605. We’re dedicated to offering quality pre-owned vehicles, and from the moment you walk through our door, we’re committed to giving you a great car-buying experience.

We work with sources across the dealer community to find a wide variety of vehicles, and we offer financing through multiple lenders. We aim to understand our local car-buying community and help customers like you find the vehicle that’s the right fit.

Browse our inventory online. If you see a vehicle you like, request a quote or contact us to schedule a test drive. We look forward to meeting you.

  • Quality brands

    We aim to stock vehicles known for their reliability and dependability.

  • Built on referrals

    Our goal is for you to be so happy with your car that you come back for the next one and tell your friends and family.

  • Credit financing

    Good credit, bad credit, or first-time buyer, we work with multiple lenders to help you get financed, subject to approval.
