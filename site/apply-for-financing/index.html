Credit application

Apply for car financing online

One secure form on our DealerCenter site. Here’s exactly what it asks for, so you can finish in one sitting. Subject to lender approval.

Apply for Financing

Good credit, bad credit, or first-time buyer, you’re welcome to apply. Subject to lender approval.

Checklist

Have these ready

The application has Buyer and Co-Buyer tabs. Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

  • Name, email, and cell phone
  • Date of birth and Social Security number
  • Driver’s license number, state, and dates
  • Current address, how long you’ve lived there, housing type, and monthly payment
  • Employer, position, employer phone, time at the job, and monthly gross income
  • Optional: the vehicle you’re interested in, vehicle price, and down payment
  • Optional: trade-in VIN, year, make, model, and mileage

Applying with a co-buyer

Switch to the Co-Buyer tab at the top of the application and enter their personal, residence, and employment details. Both applicants are reviewed together.

Adding a trade-in

Check “Add Trade-in?” on the application and enter the VIN, year, make, model, and mileage. Questions about your trade? Call (626) 764-5340.

What happens next

After you hit submit

  1. 01

    We shop lenders

    We send your application to the lenders we work with and look for options that fit your credit and budget.

  2. 02

    Go over the terms

    We walk you through the rate, term, down payment, and the full out-the-door price before you sign.

  3. 03

    Drive home

    Sign the paperwork at the dealership and drive home in your car.

FAQ

Application questions

What do I need to fill out the credit application?

Have your contact details, date of birth, Social Security number, driver’s license, current address and housing payment, and employer and monthly income handy. You can also list the vehicle you’re interested in, your down payment, and a trade-in.

Can I apply with a co-buyer?

Yes. The credit application has a separate Co-Buyer section, so you and a co-applicant can apply together.

Can I trade in my current car?

The credit application lets you add a trade-in vehicle (VIN, year, make, model, and mileage). For questions about your trade, call us at (626) 764-5340.

Can I apply with bad credit or no credit history?

Yes, you’re welcome to apply. Our lenders work with all types of credit scores and situations, including first-time buyers. Every application is reviewed by the lender, so approval isn’t guaranteed, but applying is how you find out what’s possible.
