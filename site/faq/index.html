Financing

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with multiple lenders. Fill out our online credit application and we’ll look for options that fit your situation. Financing is subject to lender approval, and rates and terms depend on the lender and your credit.

Can I apply with bad credit or no credit history?

Yes, you’re welcome to apply. Our lenders work with all types of credit scores and situations, including first-time buyers. Every application is reviewed by the lender, so approval isn’t guaranteed, but applying is how you find out what’s possible.

Can I apply with a co-buyer?

Yes. The credit application has a separate Co-Buyer section, so you and a co-applicant can apply together.

What do I need to fill out the credit application?

Have your contact details, date of birth, Social Security number, driver’s license, current address and housing payment, and employer and monthly income handy. You can also list the vehicle you’re interested in, your down payment, and a trade-in.

Can I trade in my current car?

The credit application lets you add a trade-in vehicle (VIN, year, make, model, and mileage). For questions about your trade, call us at (626) 764-5340.

Can I estimate a monthly payment?

Yes. Every vehicle page has a payment calculator. Enter a down payment, rate, and term to see an estimate. It isn’t an offer of credit; your actual terms depend on the lender and your credit.

Buying a car

Do you sell new cars?

We sell pre-owned vehicles. See everything currently on the lot on our live inventory page.

Do the listed prices include fees?

Our online listings note that dealer fees are not disclosed there, so please contact us to confirm the full out-the-door price before you buy. Taxes, title, and registration are extra.

Do your cars come with a warranty?

It depends on the vehicle, so ask us about the one you’re interested in. Every used car offered by a dealer has a federal Buyers Guide on the window that says whether it’s sold with a warranty or “as is.”

Can I test drive a car?

Yes. Call (626) 764-5340, or open any vehicle’s live listing and use “Schedule A Test Drive” or “Confirm Availability.” Each vehicle page here links to its live listing.

Can I see a car without coming in?

Yes. Every live listing has a “Virtual Appointment” request, and each vehicle page on this site links straight to it. You can also call and ask for extra photos.

Can I see a vehicle history report?

Many of our online listings include a link to an AutoCheck vehicle history report. If you don’t see one, ask us.

Why don’t some cars have photos yet?

Newly arrived vehicles sometimes go online before photos are taken. Call us and we can send pictures, book a virtual appointment, or set up a time for you to see the car in person.

What if I don’t see the car I want?

Use our Car Finder. Tell us the make, model, year, mileage, and price range you want, and we’ll help you look for it through our dealer sources, including auctions.
